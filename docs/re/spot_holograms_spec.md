## 2. Placement and scene composition (session 2026-11-16)

**The anchor is the spot's road-side entrance** [DATA: `spots.bin` record +0x28 position / +0x38 heading — the SECOND position pair; +0x10 is the building]. Verified: the Mercedes dealer of Hawai (ECD_MERC_7416) has its building point at (44452, 29811) — under the dealer's own overhang, which is why the session-31 "car parked under the overhang" read odd — and its entrance 52 m away at (44410, 29781). The port already decoded this pair for the enter press (`FindSpotEntranceById`, gm_freeride.cpp) and `-atspot`; the holograms now anchor there too (spot Id != 0; race starts and championships keep their gate/start positions). The old law (building point nudged up to 12 m toward the eye + a per-spot-hash side offset of 7 m) put the plate INSIDE the building volume — invisible at the Ferrari dealer of Ibiza from the road, clipped into the canopy at the night capture of G-Miami.

**Authored size, no distance growth** [DATA: the owner's retail capture of the owned-house marker reads the frame's authored 10 m height against the street]: `Scale = 1`. The old `1.5 * clamp(distance/60, 1, 2.2)` doubled the plates at range.

**Frame (AddOn) heading = the plate's heading** (both face the camera). The old "+pi, the backplate is drawn from behind" flip faced the frame's front away from the camera: only its pointer cone stayed visible and the square vanished.

**The frame scene decodes as** (`-logspotholo` material/node dump): pulse layers (`Spot_Pulse_*` x2, UVA scrolls) + the ring (`Square/Circle/Rhombus_*_Inside/Outside`) + one `Arrow_*` material on the flat direction pointer. The pointer's FACE is its own yaw (calibrated against the fixed-yaw test: face-on at the camera's bearing, edge-on 90 deg off). The port hides the authored-direction node (the second `EdenDrawParams::ForceHiddenNodes` path) and redraws it alone (`EdenModel::drawNode`) with the plate's heading and a roll of ±0.61 rad toward the building's door — the door bearing is the building point minus the entrance point projected on the plate's right axis (rx=cos h, rz=-sin h); the positive RotZ roll leans the tip to the plate's LEFT (verified at the Ferrari dealer, approach from the south; `-holoarrowflip` is the side A/B). The opposite approach side and the other frames' leans are not yet eyeballed in play.

**`pack_spots.bnk` loads as a scenaric pack** [CODE-dev: the dev exe loads `Scenarics\Packs\pack_spots` at 0x892250..0x8922d4 as the third pack beside worldactors and worldlifeset; `SpotMesh` is a scenaric entity class of `SCEntityFactory.cpp` — name getter 0x8a5490, class ctor 0x8a54a0/0x8a5550]. `MngSpots.cpp` / `Spot.cpp` / `Spots.cpp` are readable in the dev build only.

**The Type condition (record +144, `__StructScDataCondition { VIRTUAL if; VIRTUAL then; }`)**: the `then` word takes exactly 9 values over the 56 icon plates — 0x36, 0x3a, 0x3e, 0x42, 0x46, 0x4a, 0x4e, 0x52 (step 4) and 0xaa [DATA] — matching the schema's nine `Struct*` classes in order (House, Club, Goodies, Other, Tuning, School, DriveIn, Challenge, CarDealer): CarDealer=0xaa (all CD_*/CDC_* brand plates share the class), Challenge=0x52 (Event, Cup, Championship_*, Race/Speed/Radar_Multi, Dual, FTL/KYD_Coop, DLC_Event, Spot_Photo), School=0x4a, Tuning=0x46, Club=0x3a (Club, My_Club), and the shops spread over the rest (Clothes_Shop=0x3e; Realtor/Car_Wash/Stickers=0x42; GamblinHall=0x4e). Playerhouse/House_To_Buy share 0x36 with Photograph/Surgery/Airport/Used_Car_Dealer/Hair_Stylist/Casino, so the class alone does not pick the plate — the `if` side (two words per VIRTUAL; the second word varies per record and its meaning is NOT decoded) must carry the rest. The 8 AddOn frames store 64-bit-ish hash pairs there (`7ca75e0e 1bf4385a` for Square_Blue), not the Eden 8-slot `GetIdentifier64bit` packing — a different hash function, unresolved. The spots.bin category byte (+0x100, 0..0x19) is the map-icon category, NOT this enum. Port rule unchanged (name-prefix mapping in `HologramIconFor`), which agrees with every decoded value.

**The record also carries** `SignificanceLevel` (u32, +56: streaming/LOD significance, not yet applied) and a `Shape` u64 (+160).
